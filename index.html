个人简介About

我在海南省农业科学院三亚研究院从事热带农业智能感知与精准作业装备研究，并在海南大学和北京林业大学兼职指导博士生。我的研究面向热带农业生产与现代种业，将作物和环境感知、智能决策与控制、装备设计及作业评价贯通起来，推动农艺与农机协同。

I study intelligent sensing and precision equipment for tropical agriculture at the Sanya Research Institute of the Hainan Academy of Agricultural Sciences. I also supervise PhD students as an adjunct at Hainan University and Beijing Forestry University. My work integrates crop and environmental sensing, decision-making and control, equipment design, and field evaluation for tropical farming and modern seed production.

研究方向Research

农业多源感知Multi-source sensing

融合无人机、卫星遥感和近地传感数据，监测水稻物候与生物量、槟榔黄叶病以及热带植被变化。Integrating UAV, satellite and near-ground sensor data to monitor rice phenology and biomass, arecanut yellow leaf disease, and changes in tropical vegetation.

智能决策与控制Intelligent decision-making and control

采用图像识别、深度学习和时序数据融合，将作物与环境观测转化为可用于管理和作业的指标。Using image recognition, deep learning and time-series data fusion to turn crop and environmental observations into indicators for management and field operations.

热带农业精准作业装备Precision equipment for tropical agriculture

面向热带设施农业和现代种业，研究专用装备及关键部件，并结合田间试验评价作业效果。Developing specialized equipment and components for tropical protected cultivation and modern seed production, with field trials to evaluate performance.
