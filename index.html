个人简介Academic Profile

邱梓轩Zixuan Qiu, PhD

研究员 · 副院长Research Professor · Deputy Director

海南省农业科学院三亚研究院Sanya Research Institute, Hainan Academy of Agricultural Sciences

我研究热带农业智能感知与精准作业装备，面向热带农业生产与现代种业，开展农业多源信息感知、智能决策与控制、专用装备及关键部件研究，推动农艺农机融合。I study intelligent sensing and precision equipment for tropical agriculture and modern seed production. My work integrates multi-source sensing, intelligent decision-making and control, and the design of specialized agricultural machinery.

海南大学、北京林业大学兼职博士生导师。Adjunct PhD supervisor at Hainan University and Beijing Forestry University.
