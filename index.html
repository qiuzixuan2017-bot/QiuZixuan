邱梓轩个人简历Curriculum Vitae of Zixuan Qiu

邱梓轩，1991年生，男，黑龙江哈尔滨人，中共党员，工学博士，海南省农业科学院三亚研究院(海南省实验动物研究中心) 研究员/副院长，海南大学、北京林业大学兼职博士生导师，海南省D类人才，三亚崖州湾科技城D类人才，Agriculture Communications青年编委，西南林业大学学报青年编委，海南省科协智库专家。2014年7月毕业于哈尔滨工业大学机电工程学院，获得工学学士学位，2019年7月硕博连读毕业于北京林业大学林业装备与信息化专业，获得工学博士学位。研究兴趣是热带农业智能感知与精准作业装备，面向热带农业生产与现代种业发展需求，围绕农艺农机融合及复杂环境精准作业，开展农业多源信息感知、智能决策与控制、专用作业装备及关键部件研究，构建“感知—决策—执行—评价”一体化技术体系，推动农业装备智能化、绿色化与工程化应用。主持国家自然科学基金3项，海南重点研发计划、海南自然科学基金等6项，在REMOTE SENS ENVIRON（IF=11.4）等期刊发表论文35篇，出版专著3部，授权国内外发明专利22项，曾获“梁希林业科学技术奖技术发明奖一等奖”“北京市科学技术奖二等奖”“中国专利优秀奖”“中国发明创业成果奖一等奖”等科技奖7项。培养博士生4名，硕士生18名。Zixuan Qiu, born in 1991, is a man from Harbin, Heilongjiang, and a member of the Communist Party of China. He holds a PhD in Engineering and serves as Research Professor and Deputy Director of the Sanya Research Institute of the Hainan Academy of Agricultural Sciences (Hainan Laboratory Animal Research Center). He is an adjunct PhD supervisor at Hainan University and Beijing Forestry University, a Category D talent in Hainan Province and in Sanya Yazhou Bay Science and Technology City, an early-career editorial board member of Agriculture Communications and the Journal of Southwest Forestry University, and a think tank expert for the Hainan Association for Science and Technology. He received his Bachelor of Engineering from the School of Mechatronics Engineering at Harbin Institute of Technology in July 2014 and his PhD in Engineering in Forestry Equipment and Informatization from Beijing Forestry University in July 2019 through a combined master's and doctoral program. His research focuses on intelligent sensing and precision equipment for tropical agriculture. To meet the needs of tropical agriculture and modern seed production, he studies the integration of agronomy and machinery, precision operations in complex environments, multi-source agricultural sensing, intelligent decision-making and control, and specialized equipment and key components. This work integrates sensing, decision-making, execution, and evaluation to support intelligent, environmentally sustainable agricultural machinery and its practical application. He has led three National Natural Science Foundation of China projects and six other projects under programs including Hainan's Key R&D Program and Natural Science Foundation. He has published 35 papers in journals including Remote Sensing of Environment (IF = 11.4), published three books, and been granted 22 Chinese and international invention patents. He has received seven science and technology awards, including first prize for technological invention in the Liang Xi Forestry Science and Technology Awards, second prize in the Beijing Science and Technology Awards, the China Patent Excellence Award, and first prize in the China Invention and Entrepreneurship Achievement Awards. He has supervised four doctoral and 18 master's students.
